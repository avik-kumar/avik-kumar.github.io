## Overview

The Data subteam builds the software that lets Georgia Tech Solar Racing observe the car while it drives, analyze how it performed, and plan race strategy. I joined the team in August 2025 and have led the subteam since August 2026 [[TODO: confirm promotion date — résumé lists the lead role from Aug 2025]], coordinating a team of 10+ members across simulation, strategy, and real-time visualization.

## My contribution

- Built and deployed the team's real-time telemetry visualizer on AWS EC2 with Grafana and InfluxDB, used for live vehicle tracking, lap recording, and telemetry analysis during FSGP and ASC — more than 1,500 miles and 80 hours of driving.
- Developing a closed-loop MATLAB/Simulink vehicle simulation that couples solar generation, battery discharge and voltage, and subsystem efficiencies to characterize losses and predict realistic vehicle performance.
