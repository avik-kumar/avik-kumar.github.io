## Overview

At Ha Lab I work on the systems that collect and structure demonstration data for humanoid robot learning. The focus is teleoperation and data infrastructure for training flow-matching policies on the Unitree G1, including work in the GEAR-SONIC stack.

## My contribution

- Building teleoperation and data-collection tooling used to gather human demonstrations for policy learning on the Unitree G1.
- Supporting grasp and base-placement prior work that feeds into the lab's learning pipeline.
